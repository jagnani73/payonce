# Structure

This is one of PayOnce's design notes. [CLAUDE.md](../../CLAUDE.md) has the commands, the file map, the rules that hold everywhere and the index of these notes. Where the two differ, this file is right.

- `src/decide.ts`: pure decision policy (wait / replace / escalate / close). Rules stay in code; the model never decides amounts or moves money. `floorReason` is its cash check, which the engine also runs before it spends an approval. The reasons alerts compare with are exported constants (`ORIGINAL_DEADLINE_REASON`, the three bank-details reasons, `LAST_PAID_CHANGED_REASON` among them, `OUTSIDE_PAYOUT_REASON`, `BELOW_FLOOR_REASON`, `UNREADABLE_CASH_REASON`, `NO_FLOOR_CLAUSE` and `NOT_TO_PAY_CLAUSE`), and `engine.ts` exports `REPLACEMENT_DEADLINE_REASON` and `LATE_TRANSFER_REASON`. `OUTAGE_REASON` and `NO_FLOOR_REASON` are exported too. The two reasons supplier emails escalate for, `NOT_TO_PAY_REASON` and `PARTLY_UNREAD_REASON`, are constants too. `NOT_TO_PAY_CLAUSE` is how the ask not to pay reads when it is added to another reason, and `namingNotToPay` adds it. `LAST_PAID_CHANGED_REASON` and `UNCONFIRMED_ACCOUNT_REASON` are why a first payment waits for a person because of where PayOnce last paid the supplier. Every other reason `decide` gives is an exported constant too (`UNVERIFIED_SENDER_REASON`, `REPLACE_REASON` and the rest), apart from the one that names the source currency, and the explanation's reason grammar is built from them. The strings are the ones stored before, so no binding changed.
- `src/incident.ts`: `Incident` and `Decision` types. Amounts are minor units.
- `src/money.ts`: `toMinor`, `readMinor`, `toMajor` and `formatMoney`, for two-decimal currencies only.
- `src/engine.ts`: the agent, shared by the CLI and the server. `resumeIncident` looks at an invoice: it syncs the payments, reads new emails, decides and acts. `deliver` puts a fixture thread's emails on an invoice (uploads go through `src/intake.ts`), and `noteSenders` writes the sender and Reply-To check. `startPayment` sends a new invoice's original or holds the invoice for a person; given the account key a pay run approved, it also holds the row when the record changed or the floor would be breached (`CASH_HOLD_REASON`). `startFirst` sends an open invoice's first payment from a look: a pay run row's only when the look has the run's turn (`sendRunRow`), and any other invoice's after its first send failed. `replaceUnderLock` is the automatic replacement. `AccountChangedError` is what `requireAccount` throws. `approvePayment` records an approval of a replacement or of paying a held invoice, `closeIncident` records a person closing a paid incident, or closing without paying an invoice with nothing live at the bank, such as a held duplicate or one closed before that a payment reopened, `payAgain` puts a person's second payment to the lock, `note` writes the timeline, and `noteError` writes a failure on it: an `AirwallexError` or `AirwallexUnreachableError` as an `error` line and one console line, since its message says all there is and it repeats on every look while Airwallex is down, a `LineError` (`line-error.ts`) as a line of its own kind with its `detail` as one console line, and any other error as an `error` line with its stack trace on the console. `Bank` is the hook told about each payment sent, with its short reference, which the sandbox bank names when it cannot read the transfer. `linkOutsidePayout` ties a payout sent outside PayOnce to the invoice its reference names or nearly matches. `rateFor` takes a new exchange rate quote for a cross-currency payment or reuses one, and `checkRate` compares a transfer with the quote its approval named. `noteMismatchedTransfer` keeps a transfer under a failed attempt's request id that does not match the invoice beside that attempt, and `MISMATCHED_TRANSFER_REASON` is why the invoice then waits for a person, with `MISMATCHED_FAILED_REASON` in its place once the bank has failed every such transfer. The engine never simulates a bank outcome.
- `src/dispatcher.ts`: `Dispatcher` runs steps one invoice at a time. `wake` queues a look at an invoice, and a wake that arrives before a queued look has started is dropped. `wake(invoiceId, true)` gives the look the run's turn, so it may send a pay run row's first payment. A wake with the turn that finds a look already queued hands the turn to that look.
- `src/watcher.ts`: the fallback for webhooks. `Watcher` reads every transfer the ledger shows in flight, PayOnce's own and outside payouts tied to its invoices, and wakes the invoice when the bank has paid or failed one. It reads an outside payout in any state that is not final, `unknown` included, so one a look read in a status PayOnce does not know, such as OVERDUE, is read again, and an incident's `watching` counts it the same way. PayOnce's own include a transfer the ledger keeps failed while the bank has it in flight, and a transfer kept beside an attempt because it does not match the invoice. It also wakes an invoice whose next look is due and one with an approval nobody has acted on. Given an interval, it runs the sweep. `lastCheckedAt` is when the last check that read every payment with the bank without an error started. A read failure prints the first time, then at most once a minute while the same message repeats, with how many reads failed meanwhile. A check that fails as a whole prints the same way, with how many checks failed meanwhile, and the watcher notes whether the sweep is what failed, as it does while Airwallex cannot be reached. A sweep's failure is forgotten only by a check that succeeds with a sweep in it, or by any check that succeeds in `pnpm recover`, which has no sweep. Any other failure is forgotten by the next check that succeeds. When it is forgotten, the checks that failed since it was last printed are printed as one count ("N more checks failed before checks worked again"), so the same failure coming back later prints at once. When a check fails with another message, the old one's count is printed before the new message. A check with a failed read leaves it as it was, so the panel's "payment states checked" age grows while Airwallex cannot be reached. Given `release` (only `server.ts` passes it), it releases a waiting run whose request is approved, at most once a minute per run, and wakes a released row that has not sent, with the run's turn, one per run per check, only while the run's key is not busy: the first due row of the run in file order whose invoice has no step running (`unsentRunInvoices` reads them by run and line). Without it, as in `pnpm recover`, it never touches an unsent run row.
- `src/deadlines.ts`: how long the bank has to pay or fail a transfer, by transfer method, counted from the transfer's `created_at`. `isOverdue` treats a time it cannot read as overdue.
- `src/arrival.ts`: the route advisory. `CORRIDORS` holds the usual arrival in business days by destination country, currency, method and, for a US local payment, clearing system, from Airwallex's payout network pages for the US and Germany. `arrivalFor` builds `ArrivalTerms` (method, country, route, days, the day it counts from, the earliest and latest usual day, and the due date) in one key order, `arrivalBlock` copies one, and `ifSentOn` counts one from another day. `arrivalRisk` compares it with the due date. `dueDateProblem` checks a due date, `today` is this machine's date, and `addBusinessDays` skips Saturdays and Sundays only. `arrivalText`, `sentArrivalText` and `replacementArrivalText` write it for people. `routeText` names a route with its method when the two differ, such as "LOCAL (ACH), usually 0 to 2 business days", so the advice reads the same as the fee beside it; the terms keep `route` as it was. `arrivalAdvice` is what the page and the CLI show for one, never part of the terms. `feeSeenText` gives the fee PayOnce last recorded for a currency and method. `ROUTES` lists the route names an estimate can carry.
- `src/sweep.ts`: `sweep` lists the transfers created since the ledger started and deals with the ones PayOnce did not send. It also records a transfer listed under the request id of an attempt marked failed with none (`recordLateTransfer`), and wakes the invoice of a pending attempt whose transfer it lists, so that invoice's look records it. A transfer under the request id of an attempt marked failed that does not carry the invoice's number, amount and currency goes to `noteMismatchedTransfer` in the invoice's turn, and the invoice is woken only when the transfer is first kept or its status changes. Every listed transfer then goes to `railFailureSeen`.
- `src/outages.ts`: rail outages. `RAIL_FAILURE_CODES` (91401, 91402, 99901, 99902, with PayOnce's labels), `outageRuleFromEnv` (`OUTAGE_FAILURES` 2 to 100, default 3; `OUTAGE_WINDOW_MINUTES` 1 to 1440, default 30; a value outside falls back with a `problem`), `describeOutageRule` (the start-up line), `payeeOf` (the payee key, a hash of the account fields; see Rail outages), `railFailureSeen` (records a failure and may declare, never throws), the timeline lines (`holdLine`, `waitingLine`, `runHoldLine`, `runWaitingLine`, and the declared and end lines), `outageEnders`, `endOutage` with `EndRefusedError` (`why` is `input`, `approver`, `unknown` or `ended`), and the views `outageView` and `outagesReport`. `OutageHolding` is what an active outage holds now as the board lists it (invoices with what it holds of each, runs, and `sends`, the `OutageSend`s per currency), and `HoldingOf` works one out; `incident-board.ts` gives it as `outageHolding`, so this file has no board code. `outagesReport` and `endOutage` take a `HoldingOf`, and `outageView` takes an `OutageHolding`. `sendsText` writes the sends for the CLIs. The Outages card and `pnpm outage` read `outagesReport`, and the board builds its outages from `outageView`.
- `src/duplicates.ts`: `sameSupplier` (two supplier names, compared in NFKC with format characters dropped, case and spacing aside; people's names go through `sameName`), `sameInvoiceNumber`, `sameNumberExactly` (the same number with no letters added), `likelyDuplicates` (the other invoices a new one may be a second bill for), `sameNumberForSupplier` (the other invoices to the same supplier whose number `sameInvoiceNumber` matches, for any amount or currency), `sameBillAndNumber` (the same test for two bills not in the ledger, such as two rows of a file), and `nearlyPays` and `invoiceForPayout` (the invoice a payout sent outside PayOnce is for).
- `src/sandbox-bank.ts`: `SandboxBank` plays the bank in the sandbox. It sends a new transfer to `SENT`, pays or fails it when the scenario says so, and pays every replacement. The scripted run sends its original inside `scripting`, and the bank leaves that one to the script. Any other original gets the outcome `expectOriginal` recorded in this process, else what `outcomeOf` reads from the ledger (a pay run row's `sandbox_outcome`), else is paid. It reads each sent transfer first, trying up to four times in all on a server error or a lost connection, as the simulation calls do. A transfer that converts is left alone, because the simulator refuses to move it, and before its deadline the bank says so once. When the simulator refuses or does not answer a move, a read the bank needs fails with an `AirwallexError` or `AirwallexUnreachableError`, or `waitForTransfer` gives up on a transfer still `SCHEDULED` (`TransferWaitError`), the bank throws `SandboxBankError`. Any other error, such as a `TypeError`, is thrown unchanged, with its own message and stack trace. A `SandboxBankError` is a `LineError` of kind `bank`. Its `what` is PayOnce's words: the sandbox's simulator, which stands in for the bank, did not move transfer REF to a status this time, the bank could not read the transfer, the transfer stayed `SCHEDULED`, or the simulator does not move a transfer that converts, with the answer's HTTP status and an error code only in the form `^[A-Za-z0-9_]{1,40}$`. Its message adds `SANDBOX_BANK_AFTER` (PayOnce keeps reading the transfer, and a person hears about it if the bank has neither paid nor failed it by its deadline), and its `detail` is the error it came from. `pnpm recover`, which stops when its scripted run's bank fails, writes `what` with `SANDBOX_BANK_STOPPED` instead and prints its stopped guidance, and its `SandboxBank` writes a failure it hears through the bank hook with `SANDBOX_BANK_RECOVER`. `send` and `settle` take the transfer's short reference, and every read failure names it when the caller has it. `PAID_OUTCOME`, `STAYS_IN_FLIGHT` and `SANDBOX_OUTCOMES` (the page's bank outcomes and a run file's `sandbox_outcome` values) live in `words.ts`.
- `src/scenario.ts`: the demo payments (`SCENARIOS`), `openScenario`, `runScenario` (the scripted incident, which returns false when the invoice is still not at rest when the wait runs out) and `untilAtRest`. A held invoice stops the script before anything is sent, and no supplier emails arrive for it. When the script's first send fails, it records the scenario's outcome with `expectOriginal`, so the bank gives it to the original that the next look sends. `eur-from-usd` is the one scenario with a `sourceCurrency`.
- `src/webhooks.ts`: `WebhookReceiver` and `verifySignature`, and a private `RefusalLog`, which prints refused requests at most once a minute.
- `src/tunnel.ts`: `openTunnel` starts a cloudflared quick tunnel and reads its address from cloudflared's output.
- `src/assess.ts`: maps an Airwallex transfer to `originalStateOf` and `resendCanFix`, compares bank details (`routeKeyOf`, `NOT_ROUTING_FIELDS`, `ROUTE_SCHEME` and `knownRoute`; see Accounts), and reads the fee the payer paid (`feeOf`) and what a transfer took from the wallet before the fee (`debitOf`). `record`, the sweep, `payoutMoney`, `rateMismatch` and `conversionText` all read the debit through `debitOf`. `RESENDABLE_FAILURE_CODES` is exported for the metrics.
- `src/ledger.ts`: SQLite tables for obligations, attempts, approvals, emails, findings and events. `isExpired` and `expiryOf` say when an approval request stops covering a payment. `readSnapshot(fn)` runs reads in one deferred `BEGIN`, on a read-only ledger too, and `approvalsFor` and `callbackRecorders` (names only) give one invoice's approvals and callback recorders. `liveAtBank` says whether the bank has an attempt's payment live, whatever state the ledger gives the attempt, and `LIVE_ATTEMPT` is the same test in SQL. `withBank` is an attempt's own payment that the bank has live and has not paid, and `mismatchedLive` is a transfer under its request id that does not match the invoice and that the bank has not failed. `lateBesideLive` is the test `paidTwice` makes, and `settle` makes it too. `schemaGaps` lists the tables in `SCHEMA` and the columns in `ADDED_COLUMNS` a file lacks, read-only, and `close` closes the connection. `recon()` gives reconciliation's store on the same connection (see Reconciliation). Its tables are not in `SCHEMA`, so `schemaGaps` and the MCP server do not ask for them. `metricsRead` and `changeStamp` serve the metrics, and `ownLive`, `liveAtBank`'s test of an attempt's own payment, is exported for them. `readingSoft(fn)` runs a view's reads with stored JSON read soft. `storedStrings`, `storedList` and `storedObject` are how the row mappers, the stored findings and the cash panel's line amount read stored JSON (see Ledger).
- `src/payments.ts`: idempotent send and status sync between Airwallex and the ledger. `recordMissingFees` reads the fee of up to five failed transfers the ledger has none for, those never tried or tried longest ago first. A transfer whose fee it could not record is read again only after 10 minutes.
- `src/reserve.ts`: the reserve floors. `reserveFromEnv` reads `RESERVE_FLOORS` (currency:amount pairs, major units), `currencyFloor` gives one currency's floor and where it comes from, `floorFor` gives the floor one invoice's replacement is held to, and `describeFloors` is the start-up line. `debitCurrency` is the wallet a payment leaves: `obligation.sourceCurrency`.
- `src/cash-position.ts`: the cash panel. `cashLines` lists every amount the ledger knows about by kind, `committedMinor(ledger, currency, { invoiceId?, runId? })` adds up the promised ones the policy, the run checks and the panel all take off, leaving out the invoice or run being paid, `payRunLines` gives one `pay-run` line per row of an approved waiting run and per released row not sent yet (from `Ledger.payRunRows`), and `cashReport` works each currency down to its headroom. `lineAmount` is what a line takes from its wallet. `waitingRunLines` gives the pay rows of a waiting run that `payRunLines` leaves out (its request not approved, or expired), built the same way, `awaiting-approval` while the request is open and `awaiting-person` otherwise. `cashReport` never takes them; only the incident board does. `FIRM_KINDS` (what headroom takes off) and `WAITING_KINDS` are exported for the board's totals. `attemptLostFee` and `payoutLostFee` are the `fee-lost` test, which the metrics use too. `cashLines(ledger, now, { openOnly: true })` skips settled and rejected invoices, whose only lines are fees kept; the board reads it that way. Given `obligations`, it reads only those invoices' lines. `committedMinor` builds its sending and approved lines from `Ledger.committedRead`, which reads only the columns those lines use (see Ledger).
- `src/run-file.ts`: the pay run file. `parseRunFile` reads the CSV (RFC 4180 quotes, CRLF or LF, a BOM, blank lines skipped), refuses a file it cannot read with `FileProblem`s, and reads each row with `rowFromCells`, which a check also uses on the cells the ledger stored. `RUN_COLUMNS`, `MAX_RUN_ROWS` (50) and `MAX_RUN_FILE_CHARS` (40,000) live here. `due_date` is an optional column.
- `src/runs.ts`: pay runs. `checkRows` (GETs only), `uploadRun`, `recheckRun`, `settleRunRequest` (keeps or opens the run's request inside the transaction that wrote the check), `approveRun`, `releaseRun`, `cancelRun`, `pendingRunApproval`, `runRule`, `runBarred`, `largestTotal`, `runNote` (the run's timeline) and `runKey` (the Dispatcher key `run:N`).
- `src/currency-lock.ts`: `withCurrencyLock` holds a lease on a currency in `currency_locks` around a payment and hands the step its `Lease`. `withCurrencyLocks` takes several in code order. Waiters queue in `currency_lock_waiters` and get the lock in the order they asked. `CurrencyBusyError` means 45 seconds passed behind the payments ahead.
- `src/fx.ts`: `quoteValidity` reads `FX_QUOTE_VALIDITY` when a quote is taken, as the bank deadlines are read, and throws on a value it does not take. `describeQuotes` is the start-up line.
- `src/approval.ts`: `ApprovalTerms` is `ReplacementTerms` (action `replace`, built by `termsFor`) or `HeldPaymentTerms` (action `pay`, built by `heldTermsFor`). `RunTerms` (action `run`, built by `runTermsFor`) is a run's. `bindingOf` is SHA-256 of the terms JSON. `payTo` is the pay-to line. `FxTerms` is the quote in the terms of a payment from another currency's wallet, built by `fxTermsOf` (and `withFeeEstimate`) in one key order. `paymentAmount` and `runAmount` are the only ways to make the `PaymentAmount` the approval policy takes. `describeRate`, `markupOf`, `paidWith` and `conversionText` write rates for people, and `rateMismatch` compares a transfer with its approved quote. `terms.arrival` is the `ArrivalTerms` of an invoice with a due date, and a run row's `arrival` is there only when the file gives the row a due date. `withArrival` swaps in another estimate and keeps the key order.
- `src/bank-change.ts`: `bankChangeForm` says what a person needs to record a callback about new bank details, or why one cannot be recorded now. `recordBankChange` checks the callback, writes a `bank_changes` row, updates the beneficiary in Airwallex and reads it back. It compares with the latest original that became a transfer, or, for an invoice held before paying, with the payment `lastPaidTransfer` finds for the beneficiary. With neither, the form says why: PayOnce has not paid the supplier, or paid it only before it kept which account each payment went to.
- `src/approvers.ts`: the approval policy. `policyFromEnv` reads `APPROVERS` (name:limit pairs), `TWO_APPROVERS_ABOVE` and `APPROVAL_TTL_MINUTES`. `signoffsNeeded` says how many approvers a payment needs and why, `eligibleApprovers` who could approve it, `checkSigner` says why a person may not approve one, and `signoffProblem` checks an approval, an invoice's or a run's, against the policy as it is now. All four take a `PaymentAmount`, never a bare number. `personName` is the one check for a name a person types, `knownName` gives a stored name only when it follows that rule, and `sameName` says whether two names are one person (see Names).
- `src/emails.ts`: `EmailReader` interface (a `name` shown on the page, and `read`), `EmailFindings` (three findings), `summarise`, `unverifiedSenders` (From and Reply-To, ASCII only), `emailEvidence` (the email part of approval terms) and the fixture loader.
- `src/eml.ts`: `parseEmail` reads an uploaded raw email (bytes) into sender, Reply-To, subject, body, `styleText`, attachments (name, type, size), `notes` (fixed codes, `NOTE_TEXT`) and `partial`, or refuses it with PayOnce's own words. `cleanText`, `cleanLine`, `validAddress`, `validDomain`, `noteTexts`.
- `src/html-text.ts`: `htmlToText`, the one-pass HTML scanner, returning the text, `styleText` and whether styles or markup could draw characters PayOnce cannot read.
- `src/css-text.ts`: `scanCss`, the CSS tokenizer that takes out every string and any text that is not CSS, and applies the fail-closed rule.
- `src/intake.ts`: `takeParsePlace` (a place in the parse queue, taken before the upload's body is read; at most four wait behind the one being parsed, and `ParserBusyError` refuses the next), `parseUpload` (runs `parseEmail` in a worker thread, one upload at a time), `storeUpload`, `candidatesFor` (the invoices an upload names; it takes an `InvoiceIndex` from `invoiceIndex`, so several uploads matched against one ledger share one index), `deliverUpload` and the timeline lines for a delivery.
- `src/parse-worker.ts`: the worker thread `parseUpload` starts. It parses the one email it is sent and posts what `parseEmail` made of it.
- `src/model-service.ts`: the model services and one request to a model over plain `fetch`, with no SDK, which the reader and the explainer share. A `ModelService` is Anthropic's Messages API at the fixed `CLAUDE_URL` (`claudeService`), or an OpenAI-compatible chat completions endpoint (`chatService`, Gemini's by default). It exports `DEFAULT_CLAUDE_MODELS` (the reader's), `DEFAULT_CLAUDE_ASK_MODELS` (Ask why's), `DEFAULT_GEMINI_BASE_URL`, `DEFAULT_GEMINI_MODELS`, `ANTHROPIC_VERSION` and `REQUEST_TIMEOUT_MS`. `firstAnswer` asks each service's models in order, one request each, and returns the first answer its `parse` accepts with the model that gave it, or the tries. A refused key skips the rest of that service's models. Every error it passes on has every service's key cut out (`redactKeys`). `ModelError` is an error status. `ModelAnswerError` is an answer PayOnce cannot use: cut on its token budget (`finish_reason` `length` or `stop_reason` `max_tokens`), stopped for another reason, a refusal included, or not what was asked for. `tryOf` turns a model's failure into a `ModelTry` (`reader-error.ts`), with Anthropic's 529 read as busy like Google's 503. `serviceView` is what the page is told about a service: its models, its host and whether it is Google's free tier, never its key.
- `src/model-reader.ts`: `ModelReader` reads the emails it is given with `firstAnswer`, one request per email. The engine gives it one email at a time. `views` lists where the emails go, for the page. When every model fails, `read` throws `ModelReadError` with the tries.
- `src/reader-error.ts`: `ModelTry`, `tryText` and `triesText`, which say what each model did in fixed words that hold no text from the service (was busy (503 or 529), was over its request limit (429), refused the key (401 or 403), refused the request, had an error on its side, timed out after 8 s, cut its answer short, stopped before its answer was complete, gave an answer PayOnce could not use, could not be reached), and `ModelReadError`. It imports only `words.ts`, so the engine reads a reader failure without importing the reader.
- `src/redact.ts`: `redactKey`, which the email reader, the explainer and the Airwallex client share, and `redactKeys`, which runs it for each key the reader and the explainer hold. It imports nothing. `redactKey` cuts the key out of error text: as written, URL-encoded, as base64 or base64url at each byte alignment, and any run of 20 or more characters from one of those forms, whatever the case of its letters, since a service can echo the key upper-cased. Each cut becomes its `label`, `[key]` unless the caller gives another.
- `src/words.ts`: fixed words kept apart from the code that writes them, so `why-facts.ts` and the read-only views take them without importing the email reader or the sandbox bank. It imports nothing: `PAID_OUTCOME`, `STAYS_IN_FLIGHT`, `SANDBOX_OUTCOMES`, the sandbox bank lines' endings `SANDBOX_BANK_AFTER`, `SANDBOX_BANK_STOPPED` and `SANDBOX_BANK_RECOVER`, `ModelFailure`, `MODEL_FAILURE_WORDS`, `FAILURES_WITH_STATUS` and `TRY_WORDS_SOURCE` (the words as a pattern). Nothing the MCP server, `why-facts.ts` or `metrics.ts` imports reaches `sandbox-bank.ts`, `airwallex/simulation.ts`, `model-reader.ts` or `model-service.ts`.
- `src/line-error.ts`: `LineError`, an error whose message is a timeline line in PayOnce's words with a kind of its own, and whose `detail` only the console gets.
- `src/reader.ts`: `modelServicesFromEnv(env, use)` sets up the model services in the order they are asked: Claude from `CLAUDE_API_KEY` and `CLAUDE_MODEL`, or `CLAUDE_ASK_MODEL` when `use` is `"ask"`, as `explainerFromEnv` asks, then the chat completions service from `READER_API_KEY`, `READER_BASE_URL` and `READER_MODEL`. Each is left out without its key, so the list is empty without either. `readerFromEnv` picks `ModelReader` on them, or the keyword placeholder when there are none. `readerNamesFromEnv` gives every name the stored findings may give for whoever read an invoice's emails: the models in `CLAUDE_MODEL` and `READER_MODEL`, both services' default models and the keyword placeholder.
- `src/why-facts.ts`: the facts "Ask why" sends (see Ask why). `whyFacts(ledger, invoiceId, settings)` reads the ledger and the settings only, in one `Ledger.readSnapshot`, and returns the facts, the `legend` (alias to real value, never sent), `asOf` (the timeline's length and last line id) and how many reasons it did not recognise. `REASON_PARTS` and `WHY_PARTS` are the reason grammar, built from the exported reason constants. Each reason part carries the `ReasonCode` the metrics count a reason under when it leads, and `REASON_LEADS` exports the parts with their codes. `KNOWN_CODES` and `FAILURE_WORDS` are exported for the metrics too. `LINE_RULES` and `FIXED_LINES` are the allowlist of timeline templates, each bound to the kinds and topics PayOnce writes it with. `Aliases` numbers people and other invoices in order of first appearance.
- `src/explainer.ts`: `Explainer` (one request per model through `firstAnswer`, on the reader's services and in their order), `EXPLAIN_SYSTEM`, `promptOf` (the user message), `explainerFromEnv` (null without `CLAUDE_API_KEY` or `READER_API_KEY`), `shownAnswer` (cleans and caps the answer and swaps the aliases back), `factsKey` and `ExplainLimiter` (the page's limits and the answers it remembers). `ExplainError` holds the tries, and its message says what each model did in `triesText`'s words. `ExplainError.limited` is true when every model answered 429 or refused the key, and one or more answered 429. `Explainer.views` says where the facts go, service by service, `googleFreeTier` whether any of them is Google's endpoint, and `maxSeconds` how long an ask can take when every model times out.
- `src/keyword-reader.ts`: the fallback reader. It matches a handful of phrases.
- `src/server.ts`: `node:http` server for `web/` and the JSON API. It runs scenarios in the background, starts the watcher and connects webhooks. It loads `.env` with `loadEnvIfPresent` before it reads any setting. Its start-up lines begin with the page's address and `describeAirwallex`, the host its Airwallex calls go to, which on `OFFLINE_HOST` says no call reaches Airwallex and nothing can be paid.
- `src/env-file.ts`: `loadEnvIfPresent` loads `.env` from the working directory with `process.loadEnvFile` when there is one, and says nothing when there is none. A variable set in the shell keeps its value.
- `src/mcp.ts`: the read-only MCP server over stdio, and the only file that imports `@modelcontextprotocol/server` and `zod`. `serveStdio` picks each connection's protocol era. It parses only the lines of `<root>/.env` that set an `ENV_ALLOWED` key, finds the ledger (`PAYONCE_LEDGER`, absolute or from the repository root, else `<root>/payonce.db`), registers the eleven tools and runs each call through `answer`. `serverBalances` reads `GET /api/cash/cached` on `PORT`. See MCP server.
- `src/mcp-views.ts`: what the MCP tools answer, from the ledger alone: `incidentsView`, `incidentView`, `runsView`, `runView`, `outagesView`, `cashView` and `auditView`, in the JSON API's shapes without `busy` and `watching`. `cachedBalances` takes the balances and floors, as numbers, from the server's answer. `ToolRefusal` is a refusal in PayOnce's words. It keeps its own copy of the incident and run views, so `server.ts` is not imported. `incidentsView` gives each invoice the incident list's headline: the board's `why` while it is on the board, otherwise its latest line from `Ledger.headlines`, never an error, an email or a draft. `suppliersView` and `supplierView` call `suppliers.ts`'s own builders, the second without `inbox` and `drafts` and with `viaInvoice`, so they have no copy to drift.
- `src/mcp-text.ts`: `marked`, the pass over a finished answer that wraps every string PayOnce cannot vouch for as `{ outsideText, from }`, `phonePatterns`, `maskPhones` and `lastFour`, and `NOTICE`, the line every answer starts with. `PLAIN` lists the full paths of PayOnce's own sentences. `COUNTRY_CODE` and `SERVER_FLOORS_UNREADABLE` are shared with `mcp-views.ts`.
- `src/alerts.ts`: `alertTargetFromEnv` reads `ALERT_WEBHOOK_URL`, `ALERT_FORMAT` and `ALERT_TEST_RECEIVER_URL`, and `describeAlerts` is the start-up line. `Alerts` is the server's alert loop: `evaluate` works out the conditions that need a person from ledger fields and records them with `Ledger.recordConditions`, and `flush` claims due alerts and posts them. It counts the inbox with `Ledger.inboxWaiting` and reads nothing else of an upload. `baseReason` drops a floor clause from a reason. `reconCondition` is reconciliation's one condition.
- `src/drafts.ts`: the outbox. Six templates (`payment-sent`, `replacement-sent`, `checking`, `callback-notice`, `bank-trace`, `refund-trace`), each a facts function, a suggestion rule and a renderer. `outboxOf` gives the drafts on file as `DraftView`s, the `DraftOffer`s that fit now, and in `withheld` the messages to the supplier it holds back while the supplier asks not to pay, `createDraft` renders and stores one, `markDraft` records it copied, sent or dismissed, and `textHash` is the hash on the timeline.
- `src/alert-payloads.ts`: the alert facts per kind, `alertMessage` (title, line, fields, link), `slackPayload` and `discordPayload`, the text helpers `oneLine`, `defanged`, `slackText` and `capped`, and `utcText`.
- `src/audit-trail.ts`: how a timeline line is hashed (`canonicalEvent`, `eventHash`, `nextLedgerHead`), `chainBreaks` and `ledgerReport` to check them, and `auditExport` and `auditCsv` for the export. `canonicalRunEvent`, `runEventHash`, `runChainReport`, `runChainReports`, `runLedgerReport` and `runAuditExport` do the same for pay run timelines. `canonicalReconEvent`, `reconEventHash`, `reconChainReport` and `reconChainProblem` do it for reconciliation's one chain, and `breaksOf` takes any line with an id, a time and its two hashes. `HeadRestart`, `restartOf` and `restartRecord` read and write the record of a running hash that started again, and `restartText` words it.
- `src/recon-match.ts`: reconciliation's pure matcher (`match`), the break kinds (`BREAK_KINDS`, `clearsByItself`, `opensAtOnce`, `clearReason`, `KIND_LABELS`, `KIND_ACTIONS`), each kind's facts in one key order, `fingerprintOf`, and the wording: `breakText` and `waitingText` check every slot again as they read stored facts back. `typeWords` and `typeName` turn a `transaction_type` into PayOnce's words.
- `src/recon-store.ts`: `ReconStore`, reconciliation's tables on the ledger's connection, created by `ensure` on every writable open. `beginRun`, `failRun`, `commit` (the read, the match and the breaks in one transaction, refused for a stale run), `explain`, `breaks`, `kindTotals` and `countBreaks` (full counts), `refusedLookups`, `lastLiveAt`, `auditSnapshot`, `alertState`, `breakTimes` (each opened break's kind, state and times, for the metrics) and `refundBreaks` (the newest refund missing break on each of the transfers given, with the stored transfer, for the outbox, looked up by key on `recon_breaks_by_key`). `ReconUnknownError`, `ReconChangedError` and `ReconKindsError` are why an explanation is refused.
- `src/reconcile.ts`: one check (`readAirwallex`, `commitRun`, `reconcileOnce`, `reconcileOffline`), `windowsFor` and `replacedRange`, `airwallexTime` (Airwallex's times, the `+0800` form included, as UTC milliseconds), `movementOf` and `reconTransferOf` (every field checked), `ReconRunner` (the server's timer and "Check now"), `reconSettingFromEnv` (`RECON_EVERY_MINUTES`), `reconReport`, `movementsView` and `explainBreaks`.
- `src/incident-board.ts`: the incident board. `boardReport(ledger, filters)` ranks every invoice that is not closed, and every pay row of the run waiting for approval, into `BoardGroup`s with fixed reason text, money at risk from the cash panel's lines and totals per currency. `BOARD_GROUPS`, `GROUP_LABELS`, `BOARD_ORIGINS`, `ORIGIN_LABELS`, `isBoardGroup`, `isBoardOrigin` and `CURRENCY_PATTERN` are what the route and the CLI check filters with. `boardGroups` gives each open invoice's group, label and `why` for the incident list. Each `BoardEntry` names its `beneficiaryId` (the invoice's, or the run row's). `boardEntriesFor(ledger, obligations, beneficiaryId, now, indexOf)` builds the entries and totals for those invoices and the waiting run's pay rows to `beneficiaryId` (every pay row when it is null) without building any other entry, each as the whole board with no filter has it, for the supplier view. The run's outage and status are worked out over all its rows before the rows are picked, and `indexOf` gives the invoice index for the waiting uploads, read only when one waits. `BoardOutage`, `BoardOutageMark` and `OutageTouch` are how the board shows rail outages (see Outages on the board), and `outageHolding(ledger)` gives `outages.ts` what each active outage holds, reading the board once on its first call.
- `src/metrics.ts`: the ops metrics (see Metrics). `metricsReport(ledger, period, now)` reads once with `Ledger.metricsRead` and `ReconStore.breakTimes` in one `readSnapshot`, and `metricsOf` builds the eight tables from those rows. `reasonCode`, `REASON_LABELS`, `PERIODS`, `isPeriod` and `PERIOD_PROBLEM` live here. Nothing on a payment path imports it. `src/metrics-cli.ts` is `pnpm metrics`.
- `src/printable.ts`: what PayOnce prints to a terminal. `printable` makes one line: a control character becomes a space and a format character goes. `printableLines` does the same and keeps the line breaks. `printableJson` writes C1 controls, U+2028, U+2029, format characters and tag characters as `\uXXXX` escapes, so the JSON parses back to the same value. `printableError` gives an error with its stack trace. `oneLine` turns every control character, a line break included, into a space and keeps format characters, for text PayOnce stores: every timeline line, the server's typed notes and other free text (`text` in `server.ts`; a typed name goes to `personName` as sent instead, and that refuses any control character), and the Airwallex account name in a pay run skip reason. `say`, `sayWarning` and `sayError` print through them. Every console line PayOnce prints goes through one of the three, so Airwallex names and errors, typed names and notes, and pay run cells cannot move the cursor or write the clipboard. That includes `pnpm mcp`, which points `console.log` at stderr, so `say` writes there. `pnpm board` takes its `printable` from here. `utcTime` writes a time as every command prints one, "2026-11-03 01:49 UTC". Airwallex's short reference and failure code are stored as Airwallex sent them, so `shownReference` and `shownFailureCode` print one only in the form Airwallex gives it (`^[A-Z0-9-]{1,30}$` and `^[A-Za-z0-9_]{1,40}$`, the patterns the supplier view and the MCP answers use), and otherwise `UNSHOWN_REFERENCE` or `UNSHOWN_FAILURE_CODE`. Every command that prints either goes through them. The page's `shownReference` and `failureText` apply the same patterns to the payments card, the approval and close cards and the Outages card.
- `src/terms-shape.ts`: `readableTerms` and `readableRunTerms` check stored approval terms, an invoice's or a pay run's, field by field in the type and form each is printed in, before `pnpm approve`, `pnpm payrun show` or `pnpm payrun approve` prints them or records an approval. Terms that are not JSON, or not in their shape, get "Request N for ... holds terms PayOnce cannot read, so there is nothing to show or approve", and the command exits 1. Only these commands use it. The engine and `runs.ts` read terms without it.
- `src/suppliers.ts`: the supplier view (see Supplier view). `SUPPLIER_KEY_PATTERN`, `supplierList`, `supplierDetail`, `resolveSupplier` and the view's types. - `src/recover.ts`, `src/approve.ts`, `src/close.ts`, `src/audit.ts`, `src/cash.ts`, `src/board.ts`, `src/payrun.ts`, `src/outbox.ts`, `src/outage.ts`, `src/ask.ts`, `src/supplier.ts`, `src/recon-cli.ts`, `src/metrics-cli.ts`, `src/reset.ts`, `src/index.ts`: the CLI commands. - `src/airwallex/`: `client.ts` (login, token refresh, 30 s timeout, refuses non-sandbox hosts), `transfers.ts` (`createTransfer` sends `source_currency`, and `quote_id` only when one is given, and `waitForTransfer` throws `TransferWaitError` when a transfer does not reach a state in time), `fx.ts` (`createQuote`, `QuoteValidity`), `beneficiaries.ts` (`getBeneficiary`, `findBeneficiary`, which gives undefined for an id Airwallex does not have, `getBankDetails`, `updateBankDetails`, and `findOrCreateBeneficiary`, which every scenario uses to find its beneficiary by nickname or create it), `balances.ts` (`getBalances` and `getAvailableMinor` on the cash account only, and `BalanceCache` for the panel), `webhooks.ts` (`subscribe`), `simulation.ts` (every sandbox-only call), `history.ts` (`listBalanceHistory`: one window, to its last page, with no currency, so every wallet comes back at once; its callers keep each window to `HISTORY_WINDOW_MS`, and it throws `HistoryPagingError` past 200 pages). `SANDBOX_HOSTS` is exactly `api.sandbox.airwallex.com` and `api-demo.airwallex.com`, the two sandbox hosts Airwallex documents. `OFFLINE_HOST` is exactly `sandbox.invalid`, for running with no Airwallex at all, as the offline checks here do: the reserved `.invalid` name never resolves and no public certificate can exist for it, so every call fails as Airwallex being unreachable and the key cannot leave over TLS. The client's constructor throws unless the base URL is https on one of the three, on the default port, with no user info, path, query or fragment, and calls go to the parsed `origin`, never the text, so an empty `?` or `#`, a backslash or a tab cannot move them to another path. Nothing else is accepted, not even another `.invalid` name. The refusal is PayOnce's words: it names `AWX_BASE_URL`, the three addresses, and of the bad value only its host, and for a value with `|` in it, it says that looks like the list of choices from `.env.example` and to keep one of them. An empty `AWX_BASE_URL` is the default, as when it is unset. Every call uses `redirect: "manual"`, and a 3xx is an `AirwallexError`, so a payment stays pending, as on a 5xx. An `AirwallexError` message keeps at most 300 characters of Airwallex's words, through `cleanLine`, and the code only when it matches `^[A-Za-z0-9_.-]{1,64}$`. An error answer that is not JSON, such as the HTML page the sandbox's login answers a wrong key with, a 403, is not quoted: the message says Airwallex answered with a page that is not JSON. Its `source` goes through `cleanLine` too. Airwallex can echo what a request sent. `withoutSecrets` cuts the client id, the API key and the bearer token the request carried out of the message, the `source` and an `AirwallexUnreachableError`'s detail, as `[client id]`, `[key]` and `[token]`, in every form `redactKey` cuts, before and after `cleanLine`, and it drops a code that held one. An `AirwallexUnreachableError` keeps no `cause`, because when fetch refuses a header value its message quotes the value. None of the three then reaches a console line, a timeline line, the page, an audit export, an MCP answer, `GET /api/cash` or `recon_runs.error`. Both errors carry `request`, the method and path, and an `AirwallexUnreachableError` also the `host`. `airwallexAdvice` turns one into PayOnce's advice from those fields and the status alone, never from Airwallex's text: a 401 or 403 on `LOGIN_REQUEST` says Airwallex refused the API key and to check `AWX_CLIENT_ID` and `AWX_API_KEY` in `.env`, a 401 on any other call says Airwallex refused the key or its token for that call and to start PayOnce again, a 403 on any other call says to check the key's permissions, as for a scoped key without one, an unreachable `OFFLINE_HOST` says `AWX_BASE_URL` is the offline host, any other unreachable call, 5xx or 429 says to try again in a minute, and any other error gets none. The 502s on starting an incident and on checking a pay run file, `pnpm recover`, `pnpm payrun upload` and `check`, `pnpm cash` and `pnpm reconcile` use it, and no timeline line holds it. `withAdvice` puts it after a message with one period between them. `AirwallexClient.host` is the host every call goes to, which an `AirwallexUnreachableError` names, and `describeAirwallex` is `pnpm ui`'s start-up line naming it.
