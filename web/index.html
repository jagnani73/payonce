New incident

PayOnce sends a sandbox transfer, then applies the bank outcome above.

Leave the invoice number empty to get a new one. PayOnce holds an invoice before paying it when its number matches one already in the ledger for the same supplier and amount, apart from case, spacing, leading zeros or letters added at the end.

The person who starts an incident cannot approve its payments.

Pay run

Columns: invoice, supplier, beneficiary_id, amount and currency, plus method and sandbox_outcome if you need them. One row per invoice, 50 rows at most.

PayOnce never reads bank details from a file. It pays the account on the beneficiary record.

The person who uploads a run cannot approve it.

Supplier email

Paste the source (Show original or View source), not the text your mail client shows. Save the supplier's email itself, not a forward.

PayOnce reads the sender, subject and text. It never opens attachments, loads images or follows links. It goes to an invoice only when you pick one.

When READER_API_KEY is set, a Gemini model on Google's free tier reads uploaded mail, and Google may use free-tier content to improve its products. Keep confidential mail out, or unset the key.
